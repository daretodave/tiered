Series 8 returns twelve queens to Pinewood for the twenty-five-thousand-pound cash prize Series 6 introduced, now an established fixture rather than a surprise. RuPaul, Michelle Visage, Alan Carr and Graham Norton anchor the same panel held since Series 4, with guest judges spanning pop, film and stage across the ten-episode run. Only after the season is already airing does the BBC announce it's ending its eight-year run with the format, handing the franchise's future to WOW Presents Plus.
