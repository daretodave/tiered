Series 12 arrives as the format's most self-critical summer yet. Facing a real conversation about declining numbers, production tore up long-standing mechanics rather than adding another twist on top: the launch coupling goes in blind, the hideaway opens for anyone uncoupled, and cameras finally catch the villa at mealtimes. Maya Jama returns for a third summer in the Mallorca chair, presiding over a season built to test whether the show can still surprise itself.
