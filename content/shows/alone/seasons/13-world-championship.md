Season thirteen broke the franchise's casting mold: ten
survivalists drawn from seven countries, the first international
field the format has fielded. The location matched the ambition —
the Richardson Mountains near Aklavik, a new Northwest Territories
site distinct from the Great Slave Lake terrain of the two prior
Arctic runs. A season built to test whether the show's identity
travels as well as its landscape does.
