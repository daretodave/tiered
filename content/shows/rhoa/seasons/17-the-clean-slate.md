Season seventeen completes a second consecutive reset. Kandi Burruss and Kenya Moore, the anchors who steadied season sixteen's rebuild, are gone — Burruss by choice, Moore not brought back. Porsha Williams, Phaedra Parks, Drew Sidora, Shamea Morton Mwangi, Angela Oakley, and Kelli Ferrell return around two additions, K. Michelle and Pinky Cole, each arriving with a public profile of her own. Scotland supplies the stretch abroad. Seventeen episodes test whether a roster with zero returning anchors can hold the center.
