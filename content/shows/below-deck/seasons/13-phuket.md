Below Deck returns to Thailand for the first time since Season 7, this time anchoring in Phuket aboard a brand-new motoryacht, Azul V, replacing Season 12's St. David. Captain Kerry Titheradge enters his third season with a familiar senior team — Chief Stew Fraser Olender, Second Stew Rainbeau de Roos, and Chef Moe Vilomar all return — alongside a mostly new deck and interior roster settling into Phuket's charter waters for the first time.
