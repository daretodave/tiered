The new yacht, Azul V, replaces Season 12's St. David, giving the production a different boat to work with in the same Phuket waters the franchise visited once before. Chief Stew Fraser Olender, Second Stew Rainbeau de Roos, and Chef Moe Vilomar all return to anchor the interior and galley departments, while the deck crew and the rest of interior report in mostly new — the roster change this season actually has to prove out.
