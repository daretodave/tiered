Season 39 resets the casting after last year's crossover: thirteen
teams of first-time racers, from siblings and married couples to a
retired fire chief racing with his grandson. Every team leaves
Vancouver holding an Express Pass that must be spent by the end of
Leg 6, and a new Roadside Assistance option lets one partner cover
a Roadblock for the other. The route covers roughly 25,000 miles
across five continents, with the franchise's first stop in Albania.
