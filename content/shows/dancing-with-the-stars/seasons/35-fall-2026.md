Season thirty-five opened with the largest field the format has ever fielded: sixteen celebrity-professional pairs, tying the record held by seasons nine and thirty-one. The cast pairs a reigning Olympic champion with a reigning Paralympic champion for the first time, and includes a pro dancer competing while pregnant with her third child — a series first. Julianne Hough and Alfonso Ribeiro return for a fourth season at the desk. The finale airs in late November.
