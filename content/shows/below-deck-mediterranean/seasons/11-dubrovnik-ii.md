Below Deck Mediterranean returns to Dubrovnik for the first time since Season 2, the location that gave the franchise its high-water mark. The M/Y Akira One is new to the fleet, and so is Chef Joy Lefaucheur running the galley. Captain Sandy Yawn, Chief Stew Aesha Scott, Bosun Nathan Gallagher, and deckhand Joe Bradley return as the crew's core, working alongside a largely new deck and interior team finding their footing in familiar waters.
