Below Deck Mediterranean returned to Dubrovnik for the first time since Season 2, the location that gave the franchise its high-water mark, and the location bet paid off. The M/Y Akira One and Chef Joy Lefaucheur's galley anchored a mostly rebuilt crew around Sandy Yawn, Aesha Scott, Nathan Gallagher, and Joe Bradley. The season posted the franchise's best premiere numbers in years, and Bravo renewed it for a twelfth season before the run even finished.
