Season twelve pushes last season's stabilization even further. Craig Conover, Austen Kroll, Shep Rose, Madison LeCroy, Salley Carson, Venita Aspen, Molly O'Connell, Whitner Slagsvol, and Rodrigo Reyes all return, and Allie Newman is the only new face joining the main cast. Charley Manley steps back from full-time duty to a recurring role rather than exiting outright. Filming stays put in Charleston, where the show has always lived.
