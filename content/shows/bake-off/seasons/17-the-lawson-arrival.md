A single new judge is a bigger structural swing than it looks on paper: last time this bench changed hands, Channel 4 moved the whole format at once, and the chemistry needed a full series to settle. This time only one seat turns over, with the hosts and the other judge already settled into a fourth year together. Whether that makes Lawson's arrival land faster than Hollywood and Leith's own debut is what this run is airing to answer.
