Season forty-two brings back Cutthroat, the three-team format the
franchise first ran in 2010. Twenty-four players, split evenly
between men and women, are drafted onto three teams in Thailand,
with team dailies feeding a nomination process and one elimination
round, the Arena. It is the first flagship season released on
Paramount+ rather than MTV, one episode a week after a two-episode
opener. TJ Lavin hosts, and the prize is a share of $500,000.
