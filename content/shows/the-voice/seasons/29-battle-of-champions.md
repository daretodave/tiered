Season twenty-nine stripped the format back. Three coaches instead of four, every one a previous winner, and a ten-episode order with the live shows replaced by prerecorded rounds. Druski joined as the show's first commentator, and a Super Steal rewarded the coach who drew the most triple turns. The compression gives the season a brisk, event-like feel, but it also takes away the live-vote stretch that usually gives a Voice season its closing tension.
