Season twenty-eight is the format running on experience. Every coach had sat in a chair before, so the blind auditions moved with practiced efficiency, and the season looked for its friction in the rulebook instead. The host could send one rejected artist back for another try, singers chose their own battle opponents, and the Knockouts dropped steals for the first time since season four. Competent and watchable, without the charge of a new voice at the table.
