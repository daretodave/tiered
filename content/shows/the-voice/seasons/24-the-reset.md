Season twenty-four was the cleanest break the format had made since its debut: no original-era coach, no founding anchor. Reba McEntire brought a country credibility distinct from Shelton's, and she arrived into a panel that already knew how to compete — Legend and Stefani with long résumés, Horan in his second cycle. The Super Save gave the playoffs a new wrinkle. A transitional season that earned more attention than it received, carrying the charge a genuine reset produces.
