Season twenty-three had a built-in narrative the show couldn't ignore: Blake Shelton, the most successful coach in the format's history, coaching his final season. The farewell framing gave every coaching interaction extra weight, and the double debut of Niall Horan and Chance the Rapper gave the blind auditions fresh angles beside Kelly Clarkson's return. The format ran with the kind of transitional energy a significant departure always generates — watchable, a little elegiac, and conscious of what it was closing.
