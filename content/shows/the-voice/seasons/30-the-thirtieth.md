Season thirty puts the fourth chair back and fills two of them with newcomers: country singer Riley Green and Queen Latifah. In Cut Week, which replaces the Knockouts, each coach sends two artists straight to the live shows and the rest compete for the last spot. A First-In button lets a coach claim an artist by turning first, once per round. Live shows and real-time voting return after a fully prerecorded season.
