time-travel set and turns the franchise's own back catalog into
a weekly mechanic — the BB Time Capsule twist revives a power or

and a late Pharaoh's Jewels twist layered on top. Seventeen
houseguests, a mid-season milestone in the show's 1,000th
episode, and Julie Chen Moonves back at the helm for another
