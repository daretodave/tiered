# Editor's Canon — Alone: The Skills Challenge

## 1. Season 1 (2022)

tag: The founding season — a skills-based format departure that built something genuinely different from the parent show.
slot_argument: Each episode: one judge, three competitors, a three-day construction brief. The alumni-judge dynamic and episodic format replace Alone's endurance model with something closer to a craft competition.
community_rank_hint: rank=1 delta=0 sentiment=hold

The only entry in the canon, and it earns its placement by turning Alone's premise into something closer to a craft competition than an endurance one. Judging by former competitors rather than outside experts raises the bar: alumni have already proved they can build to survive, so their evaluation carries a credibility no celebrity panel could match. Alone tests who can endure the longest with the least; this spinoff instead tests who builds best under a hard deadline, judged by people who've done the job themselves. That inversion — expertise judging expertise, on a brief that never repeats a skill week to week — is what makes a single season read as a complete format rather than an unfinished pilot.
