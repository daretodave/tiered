Season twenty-five repeats a conceit the show already ran: every chef is under 25, the same ceiling Season Twenty used. What's new is the wrapping — returning alumni, past challenges, and a dinner event built around the milestone woven through the episodes. Michelle Tribble returns for a third straight season opposite James Avery, and the red-versus-blue kitchen runs at Foxwoods for a third year running. The anniversary trappings are the real addition here, not the age cap.
