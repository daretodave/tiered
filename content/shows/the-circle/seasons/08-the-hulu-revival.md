Season 8 moves The Circle from Netflix to Hulu, trading seven
seasons of Michelle Buteau for new host Tinx. The format shifts
too: celebrity contestants now share the cast with everyday
players, and a live, audience-facing voting layer joins the
usual in-app ratings when it comes time to decide who gets
blocked. It's the franchise's biggest structural swing yet, and
the season is still airing, too early to know if it holds the
format's original premise together.
