Season twenty-one is the Cowell-Mandel-Vergara-Mel B panel's second year together, and the shorthand shows. But this isn't just a chemistry season — for the third time in the show's history, each judge can award two Golden Buzzers instead of one, and a new Judges' Callbacks round gives select acts a second chance at the live shows. Real structural surprise on top of a settled table, which is the format at its most interesting this era.
