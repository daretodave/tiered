Season six is the calm before a change nobody watching yet knows is coming. Piers Morgan, Sharon Osbourne, and Howie Mandel run their second straight season as a trio, and the familiarity shows in the panel's settled shorthand. The audition pool stays wide, the live rounds move without surprise, and this is the last time this exact panel sits at the table — Morgan departs before the next premiere. A steady season in retrospect, defined by what follows it.
