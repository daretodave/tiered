Seattle is the first season to film in the Pacific Northwest, and it arrives with the largest cast the format has ever fielded — seven couples, up from the five-couple norm of recent seasons. The expert panel is rebuilt from three voices to two, with Paul C. Brunson and Dr. Lisa Paz taking over the matching. Group sessions, where all the couples meet with the experts together, make their return after being dropped last season.
