Season five stayed in New York, running tasks from Times Square to Ellis Island to a
New Jersey campus, and moved the show to Monday nights. The cast was the first to include
candidates from outside the United States, and Ivanka Trump and Donald Trump Jr. began
filling in at the boardroom table. Only the closing boardroom left town, airing live from
a Los Angeles theater. A founding-era season that tightened the rules rather than the
scenery.
