Season eighteen's defining story is the panel: Kendra Scott and
Rashaun Williams, both recurring guest sharks, move into
full-time seats together, pushing the full-time bench from six
to eight for the first time in the show's run. Barbara Corcoran,
Robert Herjavec, Lori Greiner, Daymond John, Daniel Lubetzky, and
Kevin O'Leary all return. The guest chair keeps pulling bigger
names too, with MrBeast, Mindy Kaling, J.J. Watt, the Foster
sisters, and UK Dragons' Den host Steven Bartlett all sitting in.
